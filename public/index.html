Help
Frequently Asked Questions
How does the AI matching work?

We score every physiotherapist on specialization fit, rating, distance and your budget, then show your top three.

Is the symptom checker a diagnosis?

No. It gives general triage guidance only. Always consult a qualified physiotherapist or doctor for a diagnosis.

What does PhysioConnect charge?

A flat platform fee of ₹80–100 per session. The physio's fee is shown up-front on every profile.

Can I cancel a booking?

Yes. Open your dashboard from the top-right menu and cancel any upcoming session.

Where is my data stored?

In this demo, accounts, bookings and tracker entries are stored only in your own browser.
